Understand your impact

What are Carbon Credits?

In CIVIX, Carbon Credits are reward points that recognize your participation in making your city cleaner and better.

How does it work?

Report civic problems such as garbage accumulation, damaged roads, or public infrastructure issues. As reports meet CIVIX's reward conditions, you earn credits.

Why do you earn them?

Your reports help bring attention to local issues. Credits recognize your participation and encourage continued community involvement.

What can you do with them?

Accumulate credits and convert eligible balances into CIVIX coupons at the defined redemption rate.
