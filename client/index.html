01 — THE INITIATIVE

A better city
begins with
being heard.

Civic problems often go unnoticed, unreported, or unresolved. CIVIX bridges that gap by giving people a shared digital space to report issues, understand priorities, and follow progress.

From damaged roads to broken streetlights, every valid report contributes to a clearer picture of what our communities need.
